Akouō

Support

Akouō is a listening prayer journal: hear God through His Word, test what you sense, and follow Him in everyday steps.

Need help? Email barrymossip@gmail.com with "Akouō Support" in the subject line. Please include your phone model and what you were doing when the problem happened.

Common questions

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen, enter your email and tap "Forgot password?" A reset link will be emailed to you. Check your junk folder if it doesn't arrive within a few minutes.

Does it work without a connection?

Yes. Entries you write offline are saved on your phone and sync to your account when you are back online.

How do I turn the daily reminder on or off?

In the iPhone app, go to Settings › Daily reminder and choose a time. If you turned notifications off for Akouō, turn them back on in your iPhone's Settings › Notifications › Akouō.

Can I use it on my phone and computer?

Yes. Sign in with the same account and your journal syncs between the iPhone app and the website.

Who can read my journal?

Only your account. Nothing is shared unless you tap Share. Read the full privacy policy.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings › Delete account. This permanently erases your account and every entry. Download a backup from Settings › Backup first if you want to keep anything.

About

Akouō (ἀκούω) is the Greek word for hear in John 10:27: "My sheep hear my voice, and I know them, and they follow me." It was created by Pastor Barry Mossip, Senior Pastor of Roseisle Pentecostal Assembly (PAOC) in Manitoba, Canada.
