# Original-word links (Greek and Hebrew)

Each `CODE.json` maps a verse (`"c:v"`) to a flat list `[n1, s1, n2, s2, ...]` over the Berean Standard Bible text in `../bsb/`: `n` is the number of letters (spaces not counted) in a group of English words, and `s` is the Strong's number of the Greek (New Testament) or Hebrew/Aramaic (Old Testament) word behind it. `0` means no original word is linked; a negative number marks a link the source flags as uncertain.

Source: the Berean Standard Bible interlinear alignment (public domain) as carried in the Threads study tool, matched to the 2022 BSB text word by word. 118 verses have no links, mostly Psalm titles where Hebrew and English verse numbers differ.
