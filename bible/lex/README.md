# Short lexicon (Greek and Hebrew)

`G0.json` to `G5.json` (Greek) and `H0.json` to `H8.json` (Hebrew and Aramaic) hold 1,000 Strong's numbers each: `{"3056": {"l": lemma, "t": transliteration, "g": gloss, "x": short definition, "n": times linked in the BSB, "e": [[English rendering, count], ...]}}`.

Credit: Data created by Tyndale House Cambridge and STEPBible.org (STEPBible-Data brief lexicons TBESG and TBESH), licensed CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Greek definitions draw on Abbott-Smith's Manual Greek Lexicon (public domain); Hebrew definitions on Brown-Driver-Briggs (public domain). Changes for Akouō: definitions shortened to about 420 characters with markup and Septuagint notes removed; counts and English renderings computed from the BSB alignment. No Dead Sea Scrolls material is included.
