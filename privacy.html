Akouō

Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026

In short: what you write in Akouō is yours. It is stored under your account so it syncs between your devices. It is never sold, never used for advertising, and nothing in the app tracks you.

Who we are

Akouō is a listening prayer journal created by Pastor Barry Mossip in Manitoba, Canada. Questions about this policy can be sent to barrymossip@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Your email address and password, to create your account and let you sign in. Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication and are never visible to anyone, including us.
  • What you write in the app: listening prayer entries, prayer requests, weighed words, steps, stones, reflections and your settings.
  • An account ID that Firebase creates so your entries stay connected to your account.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers or usage analytics. The app contains no advertising and no tracking tools.

How it is used

Your information is used only to run the app for you: signing you in, saving your journal and syncing it across your devices. It is not sold, rented or shared for marketing, and it is not used to train any artificial intelligence.

Where it is stored and who can see it

Your account and entries are stored in Google Cloud Firestore, a Google Firebase service, in Montréal, Canada. Security rules allow only your signed-in account to read or change your entries. As the administrator of the app's Firebase project, Pastor Barry Mossip could technically access the database. He will not look at your entries except to fix a problem you ask him to help with, or where required by law.

Prayer requests sometimes include details about other people. Please use discretion, such as first names only, for anything sensitive.

Sharing

Nothing leaves your journal unless you choose to share it. When you tap Share, your phone's share sheet sends only the text you see, to the person or app you pick. Confession and heart-search notes are never included in shared text.

Daily reminder

If you turn on the daily reminder in the iPhone app, the notification is scheduled on your phone. No server is involved, and you can turn it off in Settings at any time.

Other services

  • Bible passages show inside the app from the Berean Standard Bible, which is stored with the app. If you tap "Open in" your translation, the passage opens on Bible Gateway in your browser, which has its own privacy policy.
  • The website version loads fonts from Google Fonts and the Firebase library from Google's servers. The iPhone app includes these inside the app.

Keeping and deleting your information

Your entries are kept until you delete them. You can delete any entry at any time. To delete everything, go to Settings › Delete account: this permanently erases your account and every entry. You can download a backup file first from Settings › Backup.

Children

Akouō is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Email barrymossip@gmail.com. If you are in Canada, you may also contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.
